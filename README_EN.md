# Gemini and Google AI Membership Guide

This independent knowledge base explains Google AI plan selection, account ownership, billing channels, payment failures, missing entitlements, renewals and evidence collection. It separates own-account upgrades from the Gemini-specific one-year ready-made account option.

## Start with the symptom you can verify

| What you see | Start here | Stop when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You do not know which plan fits | [Plan selection](../guides/google-ai-plans/) | The target plan is available to the current account and solves a real limit |
| You want to upgrade your own Google account | [Own-account upgrade](../guides/gemini-own-account-upgrade/) | The account, existing subscription, target plan and required data are confirmed |
| The payment page reports an error | [Payment errors](../guides/gemini-payment-errors/) | You know whether the bank, Google Payments or Google Play owns the problem |
| A receipt exists but Gemini has no paid access | [Paid but not active](../guides/gemini-paid-but-not-active/) | The original purchasing account, billing channel and final order state are known |
| Renewal failed or the plan ended | [Renewal failure](../guides/gemini-renewal-failed/) | You have distinguished a failed payment, cancellation, expiry or wrong account |

Do not buy again while a charge is complete or pending, or while an active subscription still exists.

## Current plan structure

Google currently documents Google AI Plus, Google AI Pro and Google AI Ultra. Availability, storage, features, limits and prices vary by plan and region, so the live Google One or Gemini subscription screen is the source of truth.

## Public service boundary

- Own-account upgrade requirements are confirmed case by case; no fixed credential is claimed.
- Passwords, two-step verification codes and recovery codes are not requested for an own-account upgrade.
- A one-year Google AI Pro ready-made account is a separate Gemini-only delivery option. Its availability, handover process and security steps follow the live product page and pre-purchase terms.
- No API credits, SMS verification or bulk registration are offered.
- ChongGrok is not affiliated with or officially partnered with Google.

## Completion checklist

- The current Gemini account matches the purchasing account.
- The original billing channel shows a clear final order state.
- The Google One plan name and renewal date are visible when a plan is active.
- Passwords, verification codes, recovery codes and complete payment data were not shared.
